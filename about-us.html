What is Educore?

Educore is a centralized academic organization management system designed to simplify accreditation, event tracking, financial monitoring, and communication within the university ecosystem.

Accreditation Management

Streamlines digital document submission and approval workflows for academic organizations.

Event & Financial Tracking

Enables transparent budgeting, event monitoring, and automated credit/debit logging.

Smart Notifications

Centralized announcements and notification system for students, officers, and administrators.
