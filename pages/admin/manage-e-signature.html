Usage & Rules
  • Your signature is used for System Authority approvals and official records.
  • Use a clean signature with transparent background if possible.
  • Recommended: PNG, high contrast, no extra whitespace.
  • Replacing the signature will override the previous one.
Quick Tips
Best size: 800×300 (or similar wide signature format).
Background: Transparent or pure white.
Avoid: blurry photos, shadows, and colored paper.

E-Signature Help
What is this?
Your e-signature is used as a proof of authority for approvals and actions inside the System Authority module.
What can I use?
  • Upload a PNG (transparent background is best)
  • Or draw directly — it will be saved as a transparent PNG automatically
Can I replace it?
Yes. Saving a new signature replaces the current one.
